# Design Context

## Users
Eros uses MemoryOS as a personal, iPhone-first second brain. Capture thoughts and connect people, projects, conversations, and birthdays. Arabic and English are required. Everyday use should remain local-first.

## Brand Personality
Simple, quiet, and direct. The owner rejected both the first list study and all five later Atlas/Almanac/Circles/Cabinet/Focus directions. The current owner instruction points to the already-decided Capture home screen as the visual authority. Use its restraint across the app.

## Aesthetic Direction
Use designs/references/capture-sidebar-owner-reference-2026-11-07.png: white surfaces, charcoal controls, simple system-like typography, large recording control, bottom text composer, and a hidden sidebar. Preserve that image. Apply the same visual language to the other screens. New other-screen layouts still need owner review. External references and rejected variants are history, not alternative current themes.

## Design Principles
- Follow the decided home screen. Do not invent a new visual theme.
- Show ordinary content and hide secondary controls until requested.
- Keep one type family, white backgrounds, thin neutral lines, and charcoal actions.
- Keep the personal-memory use case visible through people, sources, tentative ideas, and birthdays.
- Keep review controls outside the fictional app UI.
- Use annotation feedback as evidence; do not infer a favorite.
- Preserve source attribution and distinguish inspiration from native delivery.
