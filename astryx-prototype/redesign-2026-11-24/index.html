Your memory.
Three directions.

Three ways to capture a thought, remember a person, and find a little context. Choose a direction, then try it below.

Try a small everyday task

  1. Write a thought and save it.
  2. Open the menu and find a person.
  3. Read an original source.
  4. Try recording, then pause or save.

This single file contains all three designs. It works without a server or internet. Each direction has its own sample state; switching keeps that state until you restart or reload.

Recording, import, reminders, and answers are labeled simulations. No files or microphone are accessed. The native app remains separate.
